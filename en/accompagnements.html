01 · Diagnose · the way in

Diagnostic Stratégique

An important decision to make? A strategic reading to clarify the situation, challenge your options and settle the next step.

We lay out the context, the constraints, the scenarios and the consequences of each option. The aim is to know what to do now — or why waiting is the better course.

  • A preparatory questionnaire tailored to your question
  • A one-hour strategy session
  • A written Diagnosis & Decision Note: verdict, priorities, next actions

02 · Finance

Stratégie de Financement

Understand what your situation actually allows, what is blocking it, and what to prepare before committing to the next step.

Real capacity, debt ratio, deposit, term, rental income, structure and the order of transactions: we analyse what is blocking, what can be improved and what must stay safe.

  • Analysis of the situation, and of any refusals or conflicting opinions
  • A reading of the resources, constraints and inconsistencies
  • Identification of the missing information
  • Scenarios to examine before the next transaction

Amélie & Partners works on analysis and strategic preparation, upstream of the decision. The firm neither places nor negotiates credit, and does not guarantee that it will be granted. It does not take the place of regulated professionals.

03 · Buy in Paris

Recherche immobilière à Paris

Your partner for the search, the analysis and the decision, right through to the purchase.

For a rental investment, a main home or a pied-à-terre, the engagement combines knowledge of the Paris market, a targeted search, analysis and help in deciding.

  • Setting the brief and the maximum price
  • Search, shortlisting and reading of the opportunities
  • Analysis of the price, the works, the building’s management, the potential and the risks
  • Negotiation, and coordination with the network of professionals

04 · Steer

Trajectoire Investisseur

Build a clear trajectory, weigh the options and take the decisions in the right order so that your assets move forward.

Purchase, financing, cash, resale, refinancing or reinvestment: we build a direction and an order of execution that fit your situation — from a first investment to the next phase of an established portfolio.

  • An overall reading of assets, debts and liquidity
  • Prioritising the purchases and the arbitrages
  • Scenarios for releasing and redeploying capital
  • Strategic steering within a scope agreed in advance
