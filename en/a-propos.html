An approach
born in the field.

Founded by Amélie-Thu DUONG, the firm draws on experience of property investment in Paris since 2014.

Scroll to content

Buying, financing, operating and arbitrating: that practice feeds a structured approach, a set of resources and engagements at the service of investors.

01 · The founder’s path

Building without an instruction manual.

Born in Vietnam, Amélie arrived in France at 20 without speaking French, wanting to build a life there.

After her studies she worked in banking in Paris. Alongside that permanent post, she bought her first property in 2014. She learned to analyse financing, the market, building works and legal structures — and then to connect all of it in order to decide.

Each project adds to that experience, and to the principles that guide Amélie & Partners today.

02 · The practice

The field before the talk.

An investment is rarely settled in a spreadsheet alone. It is settled in the street, in the building, in the paperwork, in the conversations with the bank, in the works — and in how the asset will live once it is bought.

The Amélie & Partners approach looks for that overall coherence: the property, the price, the debt, the cash, the operation, the risk and what comes next.

03 · Why pass it on?

Making decisions
more reachable.

Amélie & Partners sets down what the field teaches, and passes it on, so that investors understand their choices and act with method.

Free resources to clear away false beliefs. Structured methods for learning to reason. And, where the situation warrants it, tailored expertise for deciding or executing with more precision.

The aim is not for you to follow a recipe. It is for you to grasp the logic, and keep your independence.
