Your questions

Before we
meet.

Do I need to own property already?

No. The diagnostic suits a first project as much as an established portfolio. The right analysis depends on your situation, your objective and where you stand today.

My bank turned down my financing. Can you help?

Yes, where the matter calls for a strategic reading of the file: real borrowing capacity, income actually counted, deposit, term, structure, or the order of operations. Amélie & Partners does not, however, act as a mortgage broker.

Do you work only in Paris?

Property search is specialised in Paris. Questions of decision, trade-off and financing strategy can be examined case by case for projects elsewhere in France.

Might you advise me not to invest?

Yes. The aim is not to bring about a purchase, but to identify the most coherent decision. That may be to buy, to wait, to consolidate, to restructure, or to sell and redeploy.

Is the free first conversation an advisory session?

No. Those 30 minutes serve to understand what you need, establish whether Amélie & Partners can help, and define the right scope. In-depth advice begins within a paid engagement.
